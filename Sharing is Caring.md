- At the start, I only have these variables "var rumble, drone, hiss, metal, ghost" to make some simple synth sound. Rumble are just a simple background noise that you can barely hear. Drone are the "main character" and ghost, hiss and metal are just some different noise but more random ("Dust" "LFNoise1"). But after that, I want to make this project feels more spacial, so I come up with "Pan2" using SinOsc and "rev" as "FreeVerb2". Through out this code, and I use "kr" and "ar" and "|i|" (index) to make it sound random ( interms of time and pitch or frequency). Finally,I make drone detune to mimic what sound is  moving sounds like in a big space. So this projetc is basically a imitation of sound in space that makes Doppler effect.

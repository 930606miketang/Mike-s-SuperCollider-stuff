- At the start, I only have these variables "var rumble, drone, hiss, metal, ghost" to mke some simple synth sound. Rumble are just a simple background noise that you cna barely hear. Drone are the "main character" and ghost, hiss and metal are just some different noise but metal is more random ("Dust" "LFNoise1"). But after that, I want to make this project feels more spacial, so I come up with a idea that I make drone detune to mimic what sound sounds like in a big space. With "Pan2" using SinOsc and "rev" as "FreeVerb2". Through out this code, i use "kr" and "ar" and "|i|" (index) to make it sound random ( interms of time and pitch or frequency). So this projetc is basically a imitation of sound in space that makes Doppler effect.
